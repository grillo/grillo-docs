# Projects and Settings

A project holds one team's sensors, events, and members. Everything you see in Grillo Cloud belongs to your project.

Grillo creates and manages projects for you. To get a new project, or to change an existing one, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).

## Settings

Open **Settings** in the menu bar. The sections are listed on the left in two groups.

**You**

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your name and email, and the projects you belong to |
| **Notifications** | Emails sent to you. Earthquake alerts are set up on the **Alerts** page |

**Project**

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| **General** | The project name and logo |
| **Members** | Who belongs to the project and their roles, and pending invitations. See [Add Colleagues](/dashboard/organizations/managing-members) |
| **Recording & triggers** | Whether sensors record **Continuous** or **Triggered**, the project's **Trigger defaults**, and **Trigger emails**. See [Recording Mode and Thresholds](/dashboard/sensors/recording-mode) and [Trigger Alerts](/dashboard/alerts) |
| **Data** | The [real-time data export](/dashboard/data/realtime-export) to your ringserver, the [seismic data server](/dashboard/data/data-server) your sensors send to, and whether sensor locations are masked |
| **API keys** | Read-only keys for the [Data API](/dashboard/data/api) |
| **Billing** | The plan, billing method, and contract. Owners only. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing) |

The **Project** group is marked **ADMIN** because project admins manage these settings. Grillo staff also see a **Platform admin** group; customers do not.

### Changing settings

Owners and admins manage people directly in **Settings → Members**; see [Add Colleagues](/dashboard/organizations/managing-members). The other Settings sections show your project's settings but do not save changes yet. Until they do:

- Owners and admins change **Trigger defaults**, **Trigger emails**, and the **Real-time data export** on the previous dashboard's Project Settings page at [cloud.grillo.io/organization](https://cloud.grillo.io/organization).
- For anything else, including the project name and logo, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).

## Roles

Each person in a project has a role.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | See the Live map and events, but not change anything |
| **Member** | Use every page |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can, plus manage members and settings |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, plus see [Billing](/dashboard/billing) |

Owners and admins change roles in **Settings → Members**; see [Add Colleagues](/dashboard/organizations/managing-members).

## Leave a project

Go to [cloud.grillo.io/account](https://cloud.grillo.io/account). Your projects are listed there, each with a **Leave** button. Leaving removes your access to that project's sensors.
