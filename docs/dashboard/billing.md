# Billing and Account

## Billing

Project owners can open **Settings → Billing**. Admins and members see **Only project owners see billing.**

It shows:

- **Current Plan**: the project's [plan](/dashboard/plans)
- **Billing method**: **Card (Stripe)**, **Invoiced** under contract, or **Internal** (not billed)
- **Contract**: the contract's dates

### Projects paid by card

Projects billed through Stripe also show:

- **Subscription**: its status, such as **active** or **past due**, and when it renews or ends
- **Next invoice**: the amount and date
- **Payment method**: the card on file
- **Invoices**: past invoices, with their date, total, and status

To update your card or download invoices, go to [cloud.grillo.io/billing](https://cloud.grillo.io/billing) and select **Manage billing**. It opens Stripe's secure billing portal. (**Manage billing** in Settings does not open the portal yet.)

### Change your plan or contract

Grillo changes plans and contracts for you. To upgrade, renew, or change a plan, [contact Grillo](/support/contact). Prices are on the [Grillo pricing page](https://grillo.io/pricing).

## Account

Open **Settings → Profile**, or **Profile** in the account menu (the circle with your initials), to see your name and email, and the projects you belong to. To sign out, choose **Log out** in the account menu. To leave a project, go to [cloud.grillo.io/account](https://cloud.grillo.io/account) and select **Leave**.
