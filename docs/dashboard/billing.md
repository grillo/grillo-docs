# Billing and Account

## Billing

Grillo Cloud does not show billing yet. To see or change your plan or contract, update your card, or get invoices, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact). Prices are on the [Grillo pricing page](https://grillo.io/pricing).

Owners and admins can see the project's [plan](/dashboard/plans) in **Project settings → General**.

## Account

Open the account menu (your initials, top right) and choose **Your settings** to change your name, password, and sign-in methods. See [Your settings](/dashboard/account/creating-account#your-settings).

To leave a project, see [Leave a project](/dashboard/organizations#leave-a-project).

## Theme

The dashboard follows your computer's light or dark setting. To choose one for this browser, open the account menu and pick **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** under **Theme**.
