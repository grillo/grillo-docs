# What You Can Do as a Viewer or Member

Most people in a monitoring project watch the network rather than run it. In Grillo Cloud they have the **Viewer** or **Member** [role](/dashboard/organizations#roles). This page explains what that means in practice.

## You can

- **Watch the [Live map](/events/live-map).** See your stations, and earthquakes as they are detected and located.
- **Browse [Events](/events/event-catalog).** Look back over the earthquakes detected in the last 90 days, open any one for its details, and export the list as a CSV file.
- **Check [Sensors](/dashboard/sensors).** See which stations are **Online**, **Degraded**, or **Offline**, and why.
- **Look at the [Alerts](/dashboard/alerts) page** to see which alert emails were sent.
- **Download waveforms** from the [Archive](/dashboard/data/waveforms).
- **Set up your own view.** Choose your language (**EN**, **FR**, or **KR**), switch project if you belong to more than one, and log out, all from the right of the menu bar.

Members can also see who else belongs to the project in **Settings → Members**. Viewers cannot.

## You cannot

- Add, move, or change sensors, or their recording settings
- Invite or remove people, or change anyone's role
- Change the project's settings or see its billing

If you need any of these, ask your project's admin.

## What to tell your admin

Tell your project's admin when:

- A station shows **Offline** for more than a few hours. Give its station code and the time on its **Last seen**. See [Verify Sensor Status](/dashboard/sensors/sensor-status).
- A colleague needs access. Give their name, email address, and whether they should be a viewer or a member.
- Something on the Live map or in Events looks wrong. Give the Event ID and time.

For problems with the dashboard itself, see [Contact Support](/support/contact).
