# Add Colleagues to a Project

Members, admins, and owners can see who belongs to the project in **Settings → Members**, with each person's role and any pending invitations. Viewers do not see this section.

Project owners and admins add and remove people themselves. If your project has no owner or admin yet, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Select **Invite members**.
3. Enter the person's email address and choose their role: **Viewer**, **Member**, or **Admin**. See [Roles](/dashboard/organizations#roles).
4. Select **Send invite**.

The invitation appears under pending invitations. Grillo Cloud emails the person a link; they follow it and sign up with the invited address. See [Create an Account](/dashboard/account/creating-account).

## Change someone's role

In **Settings → Members**, choose a new role next to the person. You cannot change your own role.

## If an invitation does not arrive

- Ask the person to check spam or filtered mail.
- Check the address in the pending invitation. If it is wrong, select **Cancel invite** and invite the right address.
- If it still does not arrive, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).

## Removing someone

When someone leaves your team, select **Remove** next to them in **Settings → Members**. They lose access to the project's sensors and data straight away. People can also remove themselves at [cloud.grillo.io/account](https://cloud.grillo.io/account), using **Leave** next to the project.
