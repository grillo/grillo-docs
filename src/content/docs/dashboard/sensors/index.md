The **Sensors** page lists every Grillo Pulse and Grillo One in the active project, one row per station. The heading counts the sensors and how many are **online**, **late**, and **offline**.

See [Sensor Table](/dashboard/sensors/table-view) for the columns, filters, and the **Columns** menu, and [Verify Sensor Status](/dashboard/sensors/sensor-status) for what each state means.

Select a row to open the [sensor's page](/dashboard/sensors/sensor-details): its connectivity, current connection, station, hardware, and settings. A row flashes when its station detects a seismic arrival.

## Who sees Sensors

Owners, admins, and members see the **Sensors** page and every sensor's page. Viewers do not, except on the Network plan, where viewers see the list but cannot open a sensor.

## Changing sensors

| Task | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| [Add a sensor](/dashboard/sensors/adding-sensor) | Owners and admins | **Add sensor** on the Sensors page, or **Project settings → Sensors** |
| [Edit a station's name, site, and position](/dashboard/sensors/configuring-sensor) | Owners and admins | **Edit** on the sensor's page |
| Unclaim a sensor | Owners and admins | **Project settings → Sensors** |
| [Change recording mode or trigger settings](/dashboard/sensors/recording-mode) | Owners | **Settings** on the sensor's page, or **Project settings → Triggers** on Structural projects |
| [Update firmware](/dashboard/sensors/firmware-updates) | Grillo Support | [Contact Grillo Support](/support/contact) |
