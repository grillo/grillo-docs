# Stream data to your own ringserver

:::note Coming soon
Grillo Cloud will stream a project's live waveforms to a [ringserver](https://github.com/EarthScope/ringserver) you run, as miniSEED over DataLink, for SeisComP, Swarm, ObsPy, or any other SeedLink client. It is not available yet. To hear when it is, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact) with your project name and your ringserver's address.
:::

Until then:

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| Your sensors' data on your own system, live | On the Network plan, [send your sensors' data to your own server](/dashboard/data/data-server) and receive it with Grillo's open-source [coap2seis](https://github.com/grillo/coap2seis), into Earthworm or miniSEED files |
| Recorded data as miniSEED or CSV | [Download it](/dashboard/data/waveforms#download-waveforms) from **Waveforms**, an event's page, or a shaking event's page |
