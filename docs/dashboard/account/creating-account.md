# Create a Grillo Cloud account

Grillo Cloud accounts are created by invitation. You need an invitation to a project before you can sign in.

## Get an invitation

- **New customers:** Grillo creates your project and invites you by email. If you have sensors but no invitation, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).
- **Joining an existing project:** ask an owner or admin of the project to [add you](/dashboard/organizations/managing-members).

## Accept the invitation

1. Open the link in the invitation email. The page, **You're invited**, says who invited you and to which projects, and until when the link works. Invitation links work for 7 days.
2. Choose one of:
   - **Your name and a password.** The password needs at least 12 characters. Enter it twice, then select **Create account**.
   - **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft.** Your Grillo account keeps the invited email address, whichever account you choose.

Grillo Cloud signs you in and opens the project.

If you already have a Grillo account for that email, the page says so: sign in, and the project is in your project selector. If the link has expired or was already used, ask the person who invited you to select **Resend** in **Project settings → Members**.

## Sign in

Go to [cloud.grillo.io](https://cloud.grillo.io) and sign in with your email and password, or select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email, choose a new password, and select **Set password and sign in**.

A reset signs you out everywhere else.

## Your settings

Open the account menu (your initials, top right) and choose **Your settings** to:

- Change your **Name**, as others in your projects see it
- **Change password**. Your other sessions are signed out.
- Connect or disconnect Google and Microsoft under **Sign-in methods**. The last way you have to sign in cannot be disconnected.

If you sign in only with Google or Microsoft and want a password too, sign out and use **Forgot password?**.

## No projects

If the project selector says **You are not a member of any project.**, your account exists but does not belong to a project. Ask your project's owner or admin to add you, or [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).
