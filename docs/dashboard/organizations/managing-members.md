# Add Colleagues to a Project

Everyone can see who belongs to the project in **Settings → Members**, with each person's role and any pending invitations.

Project **owners** and **admins** can add, remove and change the role of people themselves. Members and viewers can't; ask an owner or admin of your project, or [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).

## Inviting someone

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Select **Invite members**.
3. Enter the person's email address.
4. Choose their role: **Viewer** (the default), **Member**, or **Admin**. See [Roles](/dashboard/organizations#roles).
5. Select **Send invite**.

The person appears in the list as **INVITED**. Grillo emails them a link; they follow it and sign up with the invited address (see [Create an Account](/dashboard/account/creating-account)). Invitations expire after 48 hours; if one runs out, cancel it and invite again.

To withdraw an invitation, select **Cancel invite** next to it.

## Changing a role

In **Settings → Members**, pick a new role in the person's **Role** column. You can't change your own role. To make someone an **Owner**, [contact Grillo Support](/support/contact).

## If an invitation does not arrive

- Ask the person to check spam or filtered mail.
- Check the address you entered. If it was wrong, cancel the invitation and send a new one.
- If it still doesn't arrive, cancel and invite again, or contact Grillo Support.

## Removing someone

When someone leaves your team, select **Remove** next to them in **Settings → Members**. They lose access to the project's sensors and data straight away. People can also remove themselves at [cloud.grillo.io/account](https://cloud.grillo.io/account), using **Leave** next to the project.
