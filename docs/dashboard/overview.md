# Dashboard

**Dashboard** is the first page you see after signing in. It summarizes the active project and refreshes itself every 60 seconds. Use the refresh button to update it sooner.

## Sensor status

Shows how many of the project's sensors are online and how many are offline. A warning appears when sensors are offline, and a critical warning when most of them are.

Open [Sensors](/dashboard/sensors) to see which ones.

## Triggers (Structural)

Projects on the [Structural plan](/modules/structural-health-monitoring) see a **Triggers** card: the number of [triggers](/dashboard/triggers) in the last 24 hours and the last 30 days, and the largest horizontal PGA of the last 30 days, with its sensor and time. Select it to open that trigger.

## Seismic events (Seismic and Enterprise)

Projects with [earthquake detection](/events) see counts of detected earthquakes for today, this week, this month, and the last 30 days, with a daily chart and the most recent events.

## System status

The system status and storage panels are for Grillo's own monitoring of the service. If the Services panel says system metrics are unavailable, nothing is wrong with your sensors; check the Sensor status panel and [Sensors](/dashboard/sensors) instead.
